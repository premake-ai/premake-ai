# Wiring (12 V DC, ~0.2 A max — no fuse needed below 1 A)

| From | Pin | To | Pin | Wire |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DC jack (rear of cap) | + (centre) | Power button | lug 1 | 22 AWG red, spade |
| Power button | lug 2 | Speed controller | V+ in (screw terminal) | 22 AWG red |
| DC jack | − (sleeve) | Speed controller | V− in (screw terminal) | 22 AWG black |
| Speed controller | Motor + | Wago A | — | 22 AWG red |
| Speed controller | Motor − | Wago B | — | 22 AWG black |
| Wago A | — | Fan | +12 V (yellow) | fan lead |
| Wago B | — | Fan | GND (black) | fan lead |
| Fan | PWM (blue), tach (green) | — | — | leave unconnected and insulate |
| Speed controller | pot plug | Knob pot (front of cap) | — | supplied cable |

Supply: 12 V 1 A UL-listed plug-in adapter, 5.5 x 2.1 mm plug.
The controller chops the fan's 12 V supply; most fans stall below ~30 %, so turn the knob up until it starts.

## Assembly
1. Top: screw the fan under the top grille disc (4 x M4x35 through disc and fan, nuts under the fan; fan label down so it blows up). Stick the speed board under the disc beside the fan with VHB.
2. Cap ring: fit the jack (rear), button (front left) and pot (front right, M7 nut), wire as above, then drop the top disc + fan onto the cap ring's inner ledge.
3. Body: drop the floor disc onto the body ring's bottom flange, stick 4 rubber feet under it, stand the filter on it between the centring ribs, lay the deck disc on the filter, press the cap on (its spigot holds the deck down on the filter).
